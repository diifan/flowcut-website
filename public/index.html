A FEW THINGS TO KNOW.

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Can I use videos I’ve already recorded?

Yes. Import videos from your library, review the recognized moments and edit them in Studio. You can also choose to edit the original recording.

Can I choose and edit the clips myself?

Yes. You decide which moments to keep. Preview your clips, trim and arrange them, add music, adjust the style and choose your export frame.

Does FlowCut judge my yoga technique?

No. Pose recognition helps organize video for editing. It is not medical advice, a safety assessment or professional coaching. Recognition can make mistakes, so review the suggested clips.

Are my practice videos uploaded for recognition?

Pose recognition runs on your device. FlowCut’s current workflow does not upload source videos for recognition or provide cloud video backup. Account services communicate online; see our Privacy Policy.
